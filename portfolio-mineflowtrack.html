MineFlowTrack

MineFlowTrack

  • Status: In Development
  • Category: Multi-tenant Field-Data Platform
  • Stack: Firebase/Firestore, Google & Apple Auth, Cloud Functions, IPFS, EAS
  • Role: Lead Software Engineer

What it is

MineFlowTrack is a standalone, multi-tenant platform for logging geological samples and mineral-processing runs. A company can use it to track its own field data, and with approval, export verified runs for on-chain attestation and data sales through AssayChain.

What I built

  • Multi-tenant data model: Per-company Firestore collections (company_profiles, run_entries) so each operator's data stays isolated and permissioned via Firestore rules.
  • Auth & onboarding: Google/Apple sign-in with self-service registration and a structured company-setup workflow.
  • Admin review workflow: A partner review queue for approving companies, resolving profit-share tiers, and linking executed agreements.
  • Attestation export pipeline: Approved runs export to a v1 schema, pin to IPFS, and get an EAS attestation on Base — making field data verifiable end-to-end.
  • Revenue model: A sliding-scale profit-share (by company revenue) split between the platform and its data partner, settled in USDC via x402.

Current status

MineFlowTrack is in active development. The multi-tenant platform, auth, company-setup flow, and attestation export pipeline are working. The next focus is hardening the admin review screen and settlement flow ahead of a wider rollout.

How it connects to AssayChain

MineFlowTrack is the source platform and AssayChain is the marketplace + attestation partner. Approved companies can export runs that AssayChain serves as attested data, with revenue shared back to the company and to MineFlowTrack.

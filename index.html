About Me

I'm a Principal Machine Learning Engineer and former CTO with 20+ years of production software experience, and I hold a B.S. in Computer Science – Software Engineering from the University of Montana. My work centers on applied AI: systems that take unstructured data, extract it reliably, score its quality honestly, and make it usable by both people and AI agents. I do that work alongside the domain teams who own the data.

Current Focus: LLM, RAG & AI Infrastructure

  • AssayChain (production): LLM pipeline that converts assay reports and drill logs into schema-normalized JSON, built with critical-minerals domain experts, with an A–D quality rubric and auditable provenance.
  • MineFlowTrack (in development): multi-tenant geological field-data platform designed for non-technical field users, with attestation-backed export and an x402 revenue model.
  • ML evaluation: I design and calibrate my own models using spatial cross-validation, and I document where they do and don't generalize.
  • AI infrastructure: LLM APIs, RAG, MCP integrations, AI agents, and agent-payable (x402) APIs.

Full-Stack Capability: React/Next.js frontends, Node.js/Python backends, Google Cloud/Firebase and AWS infrastructure, IPFS, and LLM API integration (OpenAI, Anthropic).

How I work: I start with customer discovery, prototype early, and ship to production in the customer's environment. I optimize for measurable outcomes over hype. Provenance, auditability, and agent protocols are tools I use where they add value.

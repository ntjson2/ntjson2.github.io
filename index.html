About Me

I'm a software engineer with a B.S. in Computer Science – Software Engineering (University of Montana, 2026) and 20+ years of production software experience. My recent work centers on applied AI: systems that take messy real-world data, extract it reliably, score its quality honestly, and make it usable by both humans and AI agents.

Current Focus: Applied AI & Data Systems

  • AssayChain (production): AI pipeline that turns unstructured assay reports into quality-graded JSON, with an honest A–D scoring rubric and on-chain provenance.
  • MineFlowTrack (in development): multi-tenant geological field-data platform with attestation-backed export and an x402 revenue model.
  • ML evaluation: I design and calibrate my own models using spatial cross-validation, and I document where they do and don't generalize.
  • AI infrastructure: LLM APIs, RAG, MCP integrations, AI agents, and agent-payable (x402) APIs.

Full-Stack Capability: React/Next.js frontends, Node.js/Python backends, Google Cloud/Firebase and AWS infrastructure, IPFS, and LLM API integration (OpenAI, Anthropic).

How I work: I'm pragmatic about technology — measurable results over hype, production reliability over trend-chasing. Blockchain, provenance, and agent-payable APIs are tools I apply where they genuinely add value, not the point in itself.
